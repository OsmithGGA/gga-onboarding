4. THE 30-LEAD GUARANTEE

■ THE 30-LEAD GUARANTEE

GGA guarantees the delivery of a minimum of 30 qualified leads within the 90-day contract period. If this target is not met by {ContractEndDate}, GGA will continue to provide its services at no additional charge until 30 qualified leads have been delivered. This applies to GGA's service fee only. The Client's Meta ad spend continues at their own cost during any extension period.

The guarantee is subject to the following conditions:

  • The Client maintains the agreed minimum daily ad budget of {Currency}{DailyAdBudget} per day throughout the contract
  • The Client completes all onboarding steps within 5 business days of signing this agreement
  • The Client responds to leads promptly and maintains accurate records in the GGA lead tracker

Definition of a qualified lead: A prospect who has submitted their contact details via the Client's Meta ad campaign, is located within the agreed target area, and has expressed interest in the Client's services.

GGA reserves the right to void the guarantee if the conditions above are not met.

5. CLIENT OBLIGATIONS

The Client agrees to:

  • Complete all portal onboarding steps promptly
  • Maintain the agreed daily Meta ad budget throughout the contract
  • Provide access to their Meta Business Manager as instructed
  • Respond to new leads within 5 minutes where possible
  • Keep the GGA lead tracker updated with lead statuses
  • Communicate with GGA via the agreed channel (WhatsApp or Slack) during business hours (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm)
